Privacy Policy

How R&G Garcia Painting handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Information We Collect

When you request an estimate or contact us through this website, we may collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, service type, and project details.

We also use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the website. This may include pages visited, general location, device and browser information, referral source, and interactions such as phone clicks, email clicks, quote clicks, and form submission status.

How We Use Information

We use submitted information to respond to estimate requests, answer questions, schedule work, follow up with customers, improve the website, and protect the site from spam or misuse.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted services to operate the website and receive lead requests, including Google Analytics and Google services used to process contact form submissions.

Sensitive Information

Please do not send sensitive personal information, payment details, passwords, or private access information through the website form.

Your Choices

You can contact us directly by phone or email instead of using the form. You can also control cookies through your browser settings or use Google's browser add-on for Google Analytics opt-out.

To ask about information submitted through this website, contact us at elmer.chapin@gmail.com or (612) 723-3772.
